About PDF Tools Pro

Making PDF tools accessible, free, and private for everyone

Our Mission

To provide free, easy-to-use PDF tools that respect your privacy. We believe you shouldn't have to upload sensitive documents to unknown servers just to merge or compress a PDF.

Our Story

PDF Tools Pro was created out of a simple frustration: why should you have to upload your personal documents to some random server just to perform basic PDF operations?

We realized that with modern browser technology, most PDF operations could be done entirely on your computer, without ever sending files across the internet. This approach is not only more private, but also faster and more secure.

Today, we offer 8 free PDF tools that all work directly in your browser. No uploads, no sign-ups, no waiting.

Our Technology

We use industry-standard open-source libraries to process your PDFs:

  • pdf-lib: For creating and modifying PDF documents
  • PDF.js: For reading and rendering PDF files
  • Modern JavaScript: All processing done client-side

Our website is hosted on Vercel, ensuring fast load times worldwide.
