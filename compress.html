PDF Compression FAQs

How much can I compress a PDF?

Depending on the content, you can reduce PDF size by 20-90%. PDFs with many images compress more than text-only documents.

Will compression affect PDF quality?

With "Low" compression, quality is preserved. "Medium" balances size and quality. "High" compression may slightly reduce image quality for maximum size reduction.

Is there a file size limit for compression?

For optimal performance, we recommend PDFs under 50MB. Larger files may take longer to process.
