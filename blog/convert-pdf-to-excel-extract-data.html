5. Frequently Asked Questions

Can this extract data from scanned PDFs?

Our tool works best with text-based PDFs. Scanned/image-based PDFs may produce very limited results. For scanned documents, dedicated OCR (Optical Character Recognition) software would be required.

How accurate is the data extraction?

For well-structured, text-based PDFs with clear tables, accuracy is very high. Complex layouts or merged cells may require minor manual cleanup after extraction.

Can I extract data from password-protected PDFs?

You'll need to remove the password protection first. Most PDF readers allow you to save a copy without password protection.
