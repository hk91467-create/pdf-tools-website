Can it extract data from scanned PDFs?

Our tool works best with text-based PDFs. Scanned/image-based PDFs may produce limited results. For scanned documents, OCR software would be needed.

How accurate is the data extraction?

For well-structured, text-based PDFs with clear tables, accuracy is very high. Complex layouts may require minor manual cleanup.

What's the difference between CSV and Excel format?

CSV contains only raw data (no formatting). The Excel format includes basic table styling for easier viewing in Microsoft Excel.
