5 Common PDF Problems and Their Free Solutions

📅 Published: January 22, 2024 | ⏱️ 10 min read | 📂 PDF Tips

PDFs are supposed to be a universal format that "just works." But anyone who works with documents regularly knows that's not always the case. From files that are too large to email to pages that display sideways, PDF problems can be frustrating. The good news? They're all fixable with free online tools.

😤 Problem 1: PDF File Is Too Large to Email

You've finished your document, but it's 25MB and your email server rejects anything over 10MB.

✅ Solution: Use Our Free PDF Compressor

Upload your file to our Compress PDF tool, select "High" compression, and reduce the size by up to 90%.

😤 Problem 2: Need to Extract Specific Pages from a Long PDF

You only need pages 5-7 from a 200-page document.

✅ Solution: Split the PDF

Use our Split PDF tool. Enter "5-7" in the page range field to extract just those three pages into a new PDF.

😤 Problem 3: Scanned PDF Displays Sideways or Upside Down

Someone scanned the document in the wrong orientation, and now every page is rotated incorrectly.

✅ Solution: Rotate the PDF

Our Rotate PDF tool can fix orientation in one click. Choose 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise rotation.

😤 Problem 4: Multiple PDFs Need to Be Combined

You have five separate reports that need to be submitted as a single document.

✅ Solution: Merge PDFs

Combine them instantly with our Merge PDF tool. Arrange files in order and merge with one click.

😤 Problem 5: Need Data from PDF in a Spreadsheet

Important numbers are trapped in a PDF table, and manually typing them into Excel will take hours.

✅ Solution: Convert PDF to Excel

Our PDF to Excel converter extracts data and creates downloadable CSV or Excel files automatically.

Bonus Tip: Prevent PDF Problems

  • Always optimize images before creating PDFs
  • Check page orientation when scanning
  • Name files clearly for easy organization
  • Keep original source files when possible
