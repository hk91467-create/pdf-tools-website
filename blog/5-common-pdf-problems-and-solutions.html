5 Common PDF Problems and Their Free Solutions (2026)

📅 Updated: May 09, 2026 | ⏱️ 10 min read | 📂 PDF Tips

PDFs are supposed to be a universal format that "just works." But anyone who works with documents regularly knows that's not always the case. The good news? Every common PDF problem has a free, simple solution. No expensive software required.

😤 Problem 1: PDF File Is Too Large to Email

You've finished your document, but it's 25MB and your email server rejects anything over 10MB. Now what?

✅ Solution: Compress Your PDF

Use our free PDF Compressor. Upload your file, select "High" compression, and reduce the size by up to 90%. The tool shows you exactly how much space you saved.

😤 Problem 2: Need Specific Pages from a Large PDF

You have a 200-page report but only need pages 5-7 for your meeting.

✅ Solution: Split the PDF

Use our Split PDF tool. Enter "5-7" in the page range field. The tool creates a new PDF with just those three pages. Your original file stays untouched.

😤 Problem 3: Scanned PDF Displays Sideways

Someone scanned the document in the wrong orientation, and now every page displays at a 90° angle.

✅ Solution: Rotate the PDF

Our Rotate PDF tool fixes orientation in one click. Choose 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise rotation. The fix is permanent.

😤 Problem 4: Multiple PDFs Need Combining

You have five separate reports that must be submitted as a single document.

✅ Solution: Merge PDFs

Combine them instantly with our Merge PDF tool. Drag files into the correct order and click merge. The combined document downloads in seconds.

😤 Problem 5: Data Trapped in PDF Tables

Important numbers are locked in a PDF table, and manually typing them into Excel will take hours.

✅ Solution: Convert PDF to Excel

Our PDF to Excel converter extracts data automatically. Review the preview, then download as CSV or Excel format.

Bonus: Prevention Tips

  • Optimize images before creating PDFs to avoid oversized files
  • Check orientation when scanning documents
  • Name files clearly for easy organization later
  • Keep original source files when possible for easier editing
