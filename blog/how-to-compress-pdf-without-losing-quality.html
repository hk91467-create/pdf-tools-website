Why Compress PDF Files?

  • Email-friendly: Many email servers reject attachments over 10-25MB
  • Faster uploads/downloads: Smaller files transfer quicker
  • Save storage space: Reduce cloud storage costs
  • Website optimization: Smaller PDFs load faster on websites
  • Easier sharing: Share documents effortlessly via messaging apps

Understanding PDF Compression Types

Lossless Compression

This method reduces file size without removing any data. The quality remains identical to the original. Best for text-heavy documents where clarity is essential.

Lossy Compression

This reduces size by permanently removing some data, primarily from images. The quality loss is often unnoticeable but can significantly reduce file size. Ideal for image-heavy PDFs.

Will compression make my PDF look blurry?

With Low or Medium compression, quality loss is negligible. High compression may slightly reduce image quality, but text remains perfectly sharp.
