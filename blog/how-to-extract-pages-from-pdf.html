Method: Using Our Free PDF Splitter

  1. Open the Tool: Go to our Split PDF page
  2. Upload Your PDF: Drag and drop your file
  3. Specify Pages: Enter page numbers in the input field (e.g., "1,3,5-7,10")
  4. Click Split: The extracted pages download as a new PDF
💡 Pro Tip: Use commas for individual pages (1,3,5) and hyphens for ranges (5-7). You can combine both: "1-3,5,7-10" extracts pages 1,2,3,5,7,8,9,10.

Can I split a PDF into every single page?

Yes! Enter "1-[total pages]" or simply use the range for all pages.

Does splitting affect the original PDF?

No. The original file stays intact. A new PDF is created with only your selected pages.
