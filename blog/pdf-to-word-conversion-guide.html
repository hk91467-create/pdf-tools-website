1. Why Convert PDF to Word?

  • Edit Documents: Modify text, update information, and change formatting
  • Update Old Files: Revise PDFs when original source files are lost
  • Content Extraction: Copy and reuse text for other documents or websites
  • Collaboration: Use Word's track changes and comments for team editing
  • Accessibility: Make content readable by screen readers and assistive technology
  • Template Creation: Turn PDF forms into reusable Word templates

3. What Gets Preserved (& What Doesn't)

✅ Well Preserved

  • Text content and paragraphs
  • Basic formatting (bold, italic, underlined)
  • Headings and subheadings
  • Page structure and reading order
  • Line breaks and spacing

⚠️ May Need Adjustment

  • Complex tables (may need realignment)
  • Embedded images (may not transfer)
  • Multi-column layouts
  • Custom fonts (replaced with system fonts)
  • Headers, footers, and page numbers

4. Tips for Best Conversion Results

  • Text-based PDFs convert best: Scanned/image-based PDFs may produce limited results
  • Simple layouts work better: Single-column documents convert more accurately than complex layouts
  • Review and adjust: Always proofread the converted document
  • Use .doc format: For editing in Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice
  • Use .txt format: For clean text extraction without formatting
💡 Pro Tip: For PDFs with complex tables, convert to Word first, then copy the table into Excel for better data manipulation.

Text content and basic formatting are preserved well. Complex visual layouts may need minor manual adjustments after conversion.

Absolutely. All conversion happens in your browser. Your documents are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete confidentiality.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Our tool works best with text-based PDFs. Scanned/image-based PDFs may produce limited text. For scanned documents, dedicated OCR software is recommended.
