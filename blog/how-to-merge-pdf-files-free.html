4. Common Problems and Solutions

❓ Merged PDF pages are out of order

Solution: The files merge in the order they appear in the list. Simply drag and drop files to rearrange them before clicking the merge button. The file at the top will be page one of your final document.

❓ Can't merge password-protected PDFs

Solution: You need to remove password protection first. Open the PDF in any PDF reader, choose "Save As" or "Print to PDF" without password protection, then upload the unprotected version to merge.

❓ Merged file is too large

Solution: After merging, use our free PDF Compressor to reduce the file size. You can often achieve 50-90% reduction while maintaining quality.

❓ One of my PDFs has a different page orientation

Solution: Fix orientation issues before merging using our Rotate PDF tool. Ensure all pages are in the same orientation for a professional result.
