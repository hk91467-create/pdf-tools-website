Common Problems and Solutions

Why are my merged PDF pages out of order?

The files are merged in the order they appear in the list. Simply drag and drop to rearrange before clicking merge. The file at the top will be the first page of your combined PDF.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

You'll need to remove the password protection first. Most PDF readers allow you to "Save As" without password, or you can use a PDF unlock tool.

Best Practices for Merging PDFs

  • Name files sequentially: Use names like "01-Introduction.pdf", "02-Chapter1.pdf" for automatic ordering
