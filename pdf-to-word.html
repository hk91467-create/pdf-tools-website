PDF to Word FAQs

    
Can I edit the converted Word document?

    
Yes! The output is a standard .doc file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and other word processors.

    
Will formatting be preserved?

    
Basic text formatting is preserved. Complex layouts, tables, and images may be simplified in the conversion.

    
What's the difference between .doc and .txt output?

    
Choose .doc for editable Word documents with basic formatting. Choose .txt for plain text without any formatting.
