Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Merging

How to merge PDF files for free?

Simply drag and drop your PDF files into the upload area above. Arrange them in your desired order, then click "Merge PDFs". Your combined document will download automatically. No software installation or account needed.

Is it safe to merge PDFs online?

Absolutely! All processing happens directly in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Currently, you need to remove password protection first. You can use our free PDF unlock tool once available.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

You can merge as many PDFs as you want! However, for optimal performance, we recommend merging up to 20 files at a time.
