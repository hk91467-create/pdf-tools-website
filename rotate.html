PDF Rotation FAQs

Can I rotate specific pages only?

Currently, the tool rotates all pages in your PDF. For rotating specific pages, use our Split tool first to extract those pages, rotate them, then merge back.

Will rotation affect PDF quality?

No. Rotation simply changes page orientation without altering content quality.

How do I fix a scanned PDF that's sideways?

Upload your scanned PDF and choose the appropriate rotation. The tool will permanently fix the orientation.
