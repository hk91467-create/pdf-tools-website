JPG to PDF FAQs

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, and PNG images. Each image becomes one page in the PDF in the order you upload them.

Can I arrange image order in the PDF?

Yes! Upload images in your desired order, or remove and re-add them to arrange the sequence.

Is there a limit to how many images I can convert?

No limit! However, for very large PDFs (100+ images), processing may take longer.
