PDF Splitting FAQs

How to split a PDF into separate pages?

Upload your PDF and enter the page numbers you want to extract. Use commas for individual pages (1,3,5) or hyphens for ranges (1-5,8-12).

Can I extract multiple page ranges at once?

Yes! Simply enter all pages and ranges separated by commas. For example: 1-3,5,7-10 will extract pages 1,2,3,5,7,8,9,10.

Is the original PDF modified?

No. We create a new PDF with only your selected pages. Your original file remains unchanged.
