Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

Welcome to PDF Tools Pro ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our website.

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information when you visit our website at pdf-tools-website-lemon.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use our PDF tools.

Automatically Collected Information:

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, and tools used.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device type.
  • IP Address: Used for analytics and security purposes.

2. How We Process Your Files

This is the most important part of our privacy commitment:

  • All PDF processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript.
  • Your files are never uploaded to any server.
  • Your files never leave your computer.
  • We cannot see, access, or store your PDF files or their contents.

This means your documents remain completely private and secure at all times.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. These cookies collect anonymous information about traffic and usage patterns.
  • Advertising cookies: If we display advertisements (such as Google AdSense), these services may use cookies to show relevant ads.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the website.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. Google's Advertising Policy
  • CDN Services: For delivering JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js).

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

5. How We Use Information

Any automatically collected information is used for:

  • Improving our website and tools
  • Understanding user behavior to enhance user experience
  • Displaying relevant advertisements (if applicable)
  • Ensuring website security and preventing abuse

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymous, aggregated data that cannot identify you individually with:

  • Analytics providers (like Google Analytics)
  • Advertising partners (like Google AdSense)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • No server-side file storage
  • Regular security updates
  • Minimal data collection practices

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:
